# L1204 — A canteen sale or top-up is never edited, only voided whole, once, on a second page that shows it: a sale paid from a balance returns its money, and a top-up is voided only while the balance still covers it

## Reason
The record of the day is what the cash in the drawer is counted against. A
mistake is put right by voiding the whole sale, which stays on record as
voided, with the day it was voided. Voiding is rare and cannot be undone,
so it asks first on a page of its own that shows what is about to be
voided; no list and no other page voids on one click (as L0016 for the pet
shop).

A top-up typed wrong is voided the same way. If part of it was already
spent, the void is refused: the balance would go below zero.

## What would be a regression
A sale or top-up whose amount or lines change; a second void that returns
the money again; a voided sale that disappears; a void on one click, or on
opening an address; a top-up voided after its money was spent.
