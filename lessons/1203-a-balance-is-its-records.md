# L1203 — A canteen balance is what its records add up to: top-ups in cash, less pay-outs in cash and sales paid from it, never below zero, never topped up above 1,000.00, and no top-up or sale for an account that has left

## Reason
A balance is other people's money held by the school. It must be possible
to say where every cent of it came from and went, so it changes only
through a top-up, a pay-out, a sale paid from it, or the void of one of
those two, and each is recorded and never edited. At any moment the balance
equals: top-ups that stand, less pay-outs, less the sales paid from it
that stand.

A pay-out returns up to what is there, in cash, also to an account that
has left: that is how a leaver gets the rest back. A top-up that would
take the balance above 1,000.00 is refused. The void of a sale returns its
money even above that; the ceiling is on what is taken in.

## What would be a regression
A balance that differs from what its records add up to; a balance below
zero; a field on any form that sets a balance; a top-up or pay-out that
changes after it was made; an address that changes a balance when opened.
