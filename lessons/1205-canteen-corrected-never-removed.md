# L1205 — The canteen's items and accounts are corrected and never removed: a sale keeps the names and prices of its moment, an item is marked sold out and back with one click, an account is marked as having left, and a card number belongs to one account

## Reason
Past sales point at items and accounts, so neither is ever removed. A
price or a name is corrected on the item; the sales already made keep the
name and the price they were made at. An item that has run out is marked
sold out with one click on the menu and comes back the same way: it is
done many times a day and is undone as easily, so it asks nothing. Only
sending the form does it; opening an address changes nothing. A sold-out
item is not on the till.

An account that has left is marked so and keeps its balance until it is
paid out. Two accounts with one card number would let one pupil spend
another's money, so the second is refused; an account may have no card.

## What would be a regression
A way to remove an item or an account; a past sale whose lines changed
when the menu did; an item marked sold out by opening an address; a
question page before sold out; two accounts with the same card number.
