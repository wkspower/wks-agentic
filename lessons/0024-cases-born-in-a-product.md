# L0024 — A case is born in the product that needed it, and is made general only when a second product needs it

## Reason
A lesson can be general before its case is. "Every example a form shows is
accepted" holds for any web page, but its case knows the pet shop's pages
and fields. Writing the case for products that do not exist yet means
guessing what they share, and a guess is not evidence. One product shows
what a case must do; the second shows which part of it was the product's.

So a case under a general label may be written against one product. When a
second product switches that label on, the case fails there, and that
failure is the moment, and the evidence, to make it general: move what both
products share into the case, leave the rest with each product.

## Source
The person's decision, 2026-11-19 (run 005): "Do not make any case general
in this run. The web cases stay written against the pet shop's pages."

## What would be a regression
A case rewritten to be general with only one product to show what general
means; or a second product that switches a label on and has its lesson's
case switched off, deleted or left failing instead of made general.
