# L0004 — Before building, ask the person everything foreseeable in one round, each question with a recommendation

## Reason
The person's attention is the scarce thing. One round costs one wait.
Questions held back come out later as stated limits or as a blocked run.

## Source
The pet shop runs of 2026-11-18. In the first three runs the builders asked
three questions and held the rest back as "limits", because asking was
budgeted; waiting for answers was 40 to 50% of each run. In the
wizardly-cerf run, after the documents required one round first, ten
questions were answered with a single "Ok" in four minutes.

## What would be a regression
A question after building has started that the description already made
foreseeable.
