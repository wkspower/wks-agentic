# L1307 — A request's place, category, priority, description and reporter can be corrected and nothing is ever removed: a place or a worker is marked inactive and back, and no two places have one name

## Reason
A request typed under the wrong apartment must be movable, and a record
that can be deleted is a record nobody can rely on (L0017, L0031: the
person chose the same here). A correction is a row in the history. There is
no address that removes a request, a place, a worker or a history row. An
apartment merged into another, an area closed, a contractor let go: marked
inactive, still on its pages and in the export, and active again in one
correction. An inactive place takes no new request (L1302).

Two places called "12B" are one place entered twice, so a name already
there is refused whatever its capitals.

## Source
The person, run 013, 2026-11-20: "Accepted", typed, to the sixteen recommendations asked in one round before building (answer 11). The builder's: no two places share a name.

## What would be a regression
A correction not stored or not in the history; an address that removes
anything; an inactive place or worker gone from the export or its list;
a second place stored under a name already there.
