# Queue of the root, from its units

The one queue where `enforcer`, `judge`, `second-judge`, `cycle` and `petshop`
put what no judge decides (principle 7). Each item: the options, a
recommendation, the cost of waiting. The root answers here and absorbs the
answer; only what the root itself cannot decide goes on to
[`../queue.md`](../queue.md), within the budget of [`../root.md`](../root.md).
