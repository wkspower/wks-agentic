# Queue of the root

The one queue of principle 7 for [`root.md`](root.md): everything the root
puts before the person, and nothing else. Each item is complete enough to
decide without reading the work. An answer given in the channel counts only
once it is written here; it is then absorbed (turned into a rule or a case of
a measure) before the work proceeds, and never asked again.

Attention is counted from this file: one per item, plus the time between
*asked* and *answered*.
